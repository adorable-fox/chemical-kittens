# Instructions

1.  **Prepare Potatoes**
    Peel the potatoes and boil them in salted water. Drain and mash into a smooth puree. Transfer to a bowl and let cool completely. This can be done on the previous day.

2.  **Make the Dough**
    Add the flour and the egg to the cooled potatoes. Combine the ingredients and knead gently until they form a smooth, uniform dough. It should be slightly, but not very sticky. Add more flour sparingly if needed.

3.  **Shape the Rolls**
    Divide the dough into 6 to 8 equal parts. Roll each part out on a lightly floured surface to form a long cylinder about the thickness of your thumb.

4.  **Cut the Dumplings**
    Cut the rolls into pieces about 1.5 to 2 cm wide.

5.  **Boil**
    Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Cook the kopytka in batches by carefully dropping them into the water.

6.  **Stir and Drain**
    During boiling, gently stir the water with a wooden spoon to check that the kopytka haven't stuck to the bottom. Once they float to the surface, let them simmer for about 3-5 minutes, or until soft and cooked through. Remove with a slotted spoon.

7.  **Serve**
    Serve the dumplings warm, topped with fried pieces of bacon/pancetta or onions.
